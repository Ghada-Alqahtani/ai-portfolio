About the Project

The AI Story Generator for Kids is a smart storytelling tool that combines AI-generated stories with a filtering system to help keep the content suitable for children.

Users can enter simple prompts such as characters, places, or story ideas. The system then generates an imaginative story that can be personalized with child-friendly themes and details.

To support safety, the generated text is passed through a classifier that detects inappropriate words or unsafe content before showing the final story.
